# Music and sound credits

`music_home.mp3`, `music_run.mp3`, `music_boss.mp3` and `music_map.mp3`, along with the real
(non-placeholder) sound effects listed in `mobile/assets/sfx/README.md`, come from the owner's own
earlier version of the game.

The reef ambience loop (`ambience_reef.wav`) and every remaining `.wav` sound effect are generated
placeholders, standing in until real recordings replace them.
