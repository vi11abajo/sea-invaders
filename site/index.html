04 — Why Seeker, why Solana

The phone is already the wallet

01

SKR is the currency of everything

Tickets, the shop, the Tide's revives. Nothing else to buy or bridge.

02

Sign-In With Solana and Mobile Wallet Adapter

No keys in the app. The wallet signs and sends; the server only co-signs what it has checked: a replayed score, a Seeker token read on mainnet.

03

Seeker Genesis Token, verified on chain

One token, one player. It unlocks the SEEKER badge on Home and on both boards, and the Seeker look for Octopi.

04

The program pays winners directly

No claim step. One Anchor program holds the tickets, the records, the weekly pools, the shop and the Tide.

Mobile Wallet Adapter · SIWS · Token-2022 extensions · Anchor · deterministic replay core shared by the phone and the server
